# 09 — Pitch notes (how to show it to Makka)

## Before the meeting
- Deploy a preview link (not indexed) and test it on a mid-range Android phone on mobile data.
- Record a 30–45 s vertical screen capture of the hero scroll + menu search — easy to forward on WhatsApp.
- Have the full menu search ready: ask them to search for their own signature («العتيل») live.

## Story to tell (5 minutes)
1. **548 ألف متابع ومفيش موقع** — the only web presence is a Google-Sites page of menu images. Customers can't
   search a drink, see prices or find the nearest branch.
2. **The hero**: scroll = the cocktail builds itself, layer by layer — their «طبقة ورا طبقة» craft, shown premium.
3. **The menu**: all 471 items, searchable in Arabic («كيوي» finds every kiwi drink), prices by size.
4. **Branches**: 23 branches, 5 governorates, one tap to Maps / call.
5. **One number**: 17370 everywhere, Talabat link.
6. Fast on phones, works on bad networks, no app needed.

## What we need from them to go live
Official logo & colours, recipes/descriptions, price confirmation, branch details, footage rights (see 06).

## Offer
Phase 1 (launch) scope from 07. Phase 2 upsells: ordering, English version, CMS for prices, seasonal campaigns.
